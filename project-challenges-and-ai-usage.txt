# Project Challenges and AI Usage Breakdown

This document outlines the specific challenges faced during the development of the Accredian Enterprise page clone, explicitly separating where AI tools were utilized versus where manual code editing, architectural decisions, and design directions were enforced manually.

## Where AI Helped Me

I utilized AI as a pair-programmer to accelerate repetitive and foundational tasks:
- **Project Setup & Scaffolding:** Initializing the Next.js app and configuring Tailwind CSS.
- **Asset Extraction:** Writing Python scripts (`download_logos.py`, `download_svgs.py`, `crop_logos.py`) to quickly scrape and process image assets from the original site.
- **Component Drafting:** Generating the initial layout boilerplate for sections like the 7-domain grid and feature cards.
- **GSAP Boilerplate:** Creating the foundational `useGSAP` hooks and `ScrollTrigger` setups.
- **Troubleshooting:** Diagnosing CSS overflow issues and suggesting the `overflow-x-hidden` fixes on the root layout to fix mobile responsiveness.

## Where I Modified or Improved Manually (My Own Edits and Decisions)

While AI generated the bulk structure, manual intervention, explicit architectural direction, and human design decisions were critical for the final polish. The following are areas I explicitly directed or coded myself:

### 1. Hero Page Landing Redesign
- **Theme Shift:** I manually directed the theme away from the generic blue/white to a stark, premium black-and-white theme.
- **Background & Layout:** Enforced a clean white background and constructed the layout manually referencing two specific screenshot mockups, rejecting the AI's initial generic hero suggestions.
- **Animation Philosophy:** Strictly mandated that GSAP stagger animations must happen at the **word level** rather than the character level (except in highly specific spaces) to ensure a premium, readable feel.

### 2. Track Record / Stats Section Overhaul
- **Repositioning:** Realized the flow was wrong and manually commanded the Stats section to be moved to the end of the page to act as a closing validator.
- **Structural Alignment:** Designed the vertical alignment structure: left side holding the "Track Record" subhead, and the right side displaying the statistics.
- **Number Effects:** Engineered the rising number effects, ensuring they animate continuously upon scroll until reaching the exact figures present on the main webpage.

### 3. Proven Partnerships Strategy
- **Hover Mechanics:** Initially explored interactable icons that float/enlarge on hover or float outwards in opposite positions.
- **Fallback Implementation:** Recognizing interface constraints and UX best practices, I pivoted and manually opted for a continuous full-stripe logo showcase (slideshow marquee) which proved to be a significantly cleaner interface.

### 4. Significant Usability Improvements & Fixes
- **Cursor Corrections:** Identified and fixed UX gaps, such as the Testimonials section failing to provide a `pointer` cursor on interactable elements.
- **Content Contextualization:** Addressed the lack of specific knowledge provided for the "Tailored Courses" section by manually providing explicit business context and ensuring the AI didn't hallucinate fake metrics (e.g., removing false claims like "73% productivity uplift" and writing honest curriculum descriptions).
- **Link Routing:** Manually ensured that all footer links were fully functional and redirected to actual valid pages rather than acting as dead `#` anchors.

## Summary of Technical Challenges Overcome

1. **Scroll Hijacking and GSAP Conflicts:** Integrating Lenis smooth scroll with GSAP's `ScrollTrigger` caused elements tied to `document.body` to freeze or jump. I resolved this by bypassing Lenis for specific background components and attaching raw `window.addEventListener('scroll')` logic.
2. **Panel Overlay Scroll Locking:** I fixed scroll-lock white flashes on the case study slide-in panels by targeting `document.documentElement.style.overflow = "hidden"` and injecting custom `.no-scrollbar` classes globally.
3. **Mobile Layout Overflows:** Fixed iOS Safari horizontal bouncing by enforcing a strict structural lock (`overflow-x-hidden w-full max-w-[100vw]`) on the root HTML/Body layout.
4. **Animation Pacing:** Manually parameterized the `RevealText` component to accept `duration` and `stagger` props, cutting the text animation speed in half for all sections below the fold to improve scanning speed on mobile.
