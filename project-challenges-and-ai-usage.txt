# Project Challenges and AI Usage Breakdown

This document outlines the specific challenges faced during the development of the Accredian Enterprise page clone, and distinctly separates where AI tools were utilized versus where manual code editing and decision-making took over.

## Problems Faced During Development

1. **Scroll Hijacking and GSAP Conflicts:** 
   Integrating Lenis smooth scroll with GSAP's `ScrollTrigger` caused significant issues, especially with elements tied to `document.body` scrolling. When building the interactive background falling lines, the virtualized scroll events from Lenis would break the expected scroll triggers, causing elements to freeze or jump.
   *Resolution:* Bypassed Lenis for the specific background line component by attaching raw `window.addEventListener('scroll')` logic to handle the precise fall mapping of the lines.

2. **Panel Overlay Scroll Locking:** 
   When opening the "Case Study" slide-in panel, the background page needed to freeze without causing a visual "jump" to the top of the screen (a common side effect of `overflow: hidden` on the body).
   *Resolution:* Initially tried saving `scrollY` and applying `position: fixed`, but this caused a white flash/background rendering issue. Finally resolved it by applying `overflow: hidden` strictly to the `<html>` and `<body>` tags while ensuring the panel itself had a hidden, independent scrollbar (`no-scrollbar` class applied globally).

3. **Mobile Layout Overflows:** 
   Mobile browsers (specifically iOS Safari) would allow horizontal bouncing due to fixed-width SVGs and complex flex layouts getting rendered slightly out of bounds.
   *Resolution:* Enforced a strict structural lock (`overflow-x-hidden w-full max-w-[100vw]`) on the root HTML/Body layout to guarantee rigid vertical-only scrolling on devices.

4. **Animation Timing on Mobile vs Desktop:** 
   GSAP animations felt too slow towards the bottom of the page, causing a dragging user experience for users wanting to scan quickly.
   *Resolution:* Parameterized the `RevealText` component to accept `duration` and `stagger` props, manually cutting the speed in half for all sections below the fold.

## Where AI Helped Me

I heavily utilized AI as a pair-programmer to accelerate development:
- **Project Setup & Scaffolding:** Initializing the Next.js app and configuring Tailwind.
- **Asset Extraction:** Writing Python scripts (`download_logos.py`, `download_svgs.py`, `crop_logos.py`) to quickly scrape and process image assets from the original site.
- **Component Drafting:** Generating the initial layout code for complex sections like the 7-domain grid, feature cards, and the footer links structure.
- **GSAP Boilerplate:** Creating the foundational `useGSAP` hooks and `ScrollTrigger` setups.
- **Troubleshooting:** The AI was highly effective at diagnosing CSS overflow issues and suggesting the `overflow-x-hidden` fixes on the root layout.

## Where I Modified or Improved Manually (My Own Edits)

While AI generated the bulk structure, manual intervention, code editing, and human design decisions were critical for the final polish:

1. **Custom Animation Tuning:** I manually parameterized the `RevealText` component to control exact duration and stagger timings (0.6s and 0.02s) to fix pacing issues the AI originally missed.
2. **Scroll Lock Logic Refactoring:** The AI's initial attempt at scroll locking for the case study panel used `body { position: fixed }`, which broke the background rendering. I manually directed the switch to `document.documentElement.style.overflow = "hidden"` and integrated the `no-scrollbar` CSS utilities.
3. **Background Lines Engineering:** I recognized that standard GSAP ScrollTriggers were conflicting with Lenis. I manually dictated the approach to use raw `window.scrollY` event listeners tied to state updates to ensure the blue lines fell perfectly proportional to user scroll without overlapping content cards.
4. **Content Auditing:** The AI attempted to hallucinate case study metrics (e.g., "73% productivity uplift"). I manually audited the text and rewrote the content to reflect honest, contextual descriptions of the curriculum rather than fake statistics.
5. **Design System Adherence:** I manually enforced strict adherence to the beige (`#F9F6F0`) and dark slate (`#0F172A`) color palette, ensuring the AI didn't drift into generic Tailwind colors (like standard blue or red).
